# 🦞 Slack Tide Lobster Shack | Small Business Website
 
A one-page website for **Slack Tide Lobster Shack**, a family-run lobster shack on the wharf in Harpswell, Maine. The site gives customers what they need before they visit: the menu, how to eat a whole lobster, seasonal hours, location, and what to know before they go.
 
 
🔗 **Live Site:** [smbusinessmn.netlify.app](https://smbusinessmn.netlify.app/)

## ✨ What's on the Page
 
| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Header** | Shack name, navigation, phone number, and a strip with the season, picnic tables, and BYOB |
| **Hero** | "Off the Boat. Onto the Roll." over a lobster roll photo, with buttons to the menu and the how-to guide |
| **Menu** | Two columns, "From the Water" and "Sides & Sweets," with market price items like the lobster roll and fixed-price sides like wild blueberry pie |
| **Lobster How-To** | A friendly 4-step guide to eating a whole lobster, for first-timers |
| **Find the Wharf** | Season and hours, address and phone, and a "Know Before You Go" card (cash or check only, dogs welcome, parking tips) |
| **Footer** | Shack story, hours, address, and a back-to-top link |
 
---
 
## 🎨 Design
 
- **Fonts:** Lilita One for headings and Nunito Sans for body text
- **Voice:** short, local, and a little funny ("Cash or check - the card reader hates fog"), to match a small family business
- **Responsive layout** with breakpoints at 1024px, 700px, and 420px for tablets and phones
---
 
## 🛠️ Tech Stack
 
- HTML5
- CSS3 (Flexbox, Grid, media queries)
- Hosted on **Netlify**
No JavaScript, frameworks, or build tools.
